Block: stand still or hold back blocks highs and mids. Hold down-back to block lows.

Crouch ducks highs and throws. Mids hit crouching players. Jump over lows.

Throws beat blocking. Break a left throw with 1, a right throw with 2, within 14 frames. A wrong press locks you out.

Dash: tap forward twice. Backdash: tap back twice. Hitting someone during their attack's startup is a counter hit.
