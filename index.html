Directions change attacks: forward, back, down, down-forward, down-back + A or K. Repeat A or K for strings. Tap forward twice to dash, back twice to retreat. Jump + A/K attacks in the air.

Guard: standing still or holding back blocks highs and mids. Hold down-back to block lows. Down alone ducks highs and throws but does not block. Mids hit crouchers.

Throws beat guard. Break with A+K within 14 frames.

Criticals are earned: precision moves landing in a punish window deal 1.35×. Head hits add 1.15×. Counter hits (during startup) 1.2×.

Knocked down? Up/A: rise · Back: roll away · K: wake-up kick.
